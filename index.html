Meet Our Founders

Two Founders. One Vision. A Legacy in the Making.

Masonia Group was founded on a shared ambition by Saimon and Ruqayyah — a vision to build a technology company that creates meaningful solutions, delivers lasting value and makes a positive impact across industries.

United by a commitment to innovation, entrepreneurship and long-term growth, they are building Masonia Group on the principles of ambition, integrity and purposeful innovation.

Their vision goes beyond developing software. Together, they aspire to establish a globally recognised technology group known for solving complex business challenges and building products that stand the test of time.

Saimon Rashid

Co-Founder & CEO

Saimon leads the strategic vision and business direction of Masonia Group. With a passion for entrepreneurship, technology and business development, his focus is on identifying valuable market opportunities, developing innovative solutions and laying the foundations for sustainable growth.

Driven by long-term ambition, Saimon aims to build Masonia Group into a respected technology company that creates measurable value for businesses worldwide.

Ruqayyah Ahmed

Co-Founder & Director

Ruqayyah plays an integral role in shaping Masonia Group's development, contributing to market research, strategic planning and the identification of business opportunities.

Her focus on research, collaboration and thoughtful decision-making supports the company's commitment to developing solutions grounded in genuine customer needs.

Together, Saimon and Ruqayyah share a commitment to building Masonia Group with purpose, ambition and a long-term perspective.
